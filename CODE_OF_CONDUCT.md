# Code of Conduct

ARC Studio is built in public. This code applies to people using the project’s GitHub spaces, the ARC Studio Discord server, and events connected to the project.

## How we treat each other

We welcome people who want to help, whatever their background or level of experience. Please be patient, respectful, and act in Good faith

Keep feedback about the work, not personal attacks. Give people room to ask questions, learn, and disagree without being rude

## What is not okay

Harassment, threats, hate speech, or personal attacks are not okay. Neither is unwanted sexual attention, sharing private information without permission, or repeatedly disrupting a discussion

These rules apply to maintainers too.

## Reporting a problem

If something happens, contact a project maintainer privately through GitHub or the ARC Studio Discord. Please do not post private reports in public issues

Share what happened and where it happened. A link or screenshot can help, but only share private details with the maintainers who need them.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers will review reports and choose a fair response. They may ask someone to stop, remove content, pause their access, or ban them from project spaces

They should explain their decision when it is safe and useful to do so. They may act sooner when someone is at risk.

Thanks for helping make ARC Studio a place where people can work together.
